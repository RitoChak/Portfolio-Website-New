About Me

From Physical Systems to Complex Data Ecosystems

My academic path started in physical sciences, earning my B.Sc. from Vidyasagar College (Calcutta University) and an M.Sc. in Physics with a specialization in Solid State Physics from Osmania University. Deciphering underlying structures in data mirrored how I learned to understand equations of state in the universe—sparking my deep pivot into Data Science.

I work across Python workflows, Machine Learning architectures, Deep Learning models, and Natural Language Processing algorithms. Over time, I have engineered custom pipelines utilizing standard ecosystems like scikit-learn, pandas, numpy, and matplotlib alongside databases (SQL) and professional visual toolsets (Tableau, Power BI).

🔬 Research & Publications

During my Research Internship at IIT Kharagpur under Prof. Animesh Mukherjee, I contributed to cross-lingual natural language optimization architectures which yielded a peer-reviewed paper:

"On the Effective Transfer of Knowledge from English to Hindi Wikipedia"
Paramita Das, Amartya Roy, Ritabrata Chakraborty, Animesh Mukherjee — Published at COLING 2025

Currently continuing collaboration targeting Bias Removal in Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and exploring multimodal AI paradigms.
